only when it is volatile execution state, or when the destination cannot
recover it cheaply from its owning source.

## Rules

- No secrets, tokens, `.env` values, or personal rosters.
- Claim pushed, merged, published, or verified only for states you verified
  this session.
- A short chat summary is enough for small work. Use this for real continuation.

**Done when** a cold agent can name the goal, the verified state, the blocker,
and the first next command without this chat.
